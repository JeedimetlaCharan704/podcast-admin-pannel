================================================================================
                    FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)
            Prepare answers for these questions before presenting
================================================================================

Q1: Is the app available on iPhone/iOS?
A: Currently, Aurora Podcast is available only for Android devices. iOS version
   is under development and will be released soon.

Q2: Is it free for all students?
A: Yes, the app is completely free for all students and staff of Aurora
   University.

Q3: How do I get an access key?
A: Visit the admin office in the main building with your college ID card.
   They will provide you with a unique access key.

Q4: Can I share the app with my friends outside college?
A: No, the app is exclusively for Aurora University students and staff.
   Only users with valid access keys can use the app.

Q5: What happens to my data?
A: We do not collect any personal data. The app only stores your listening
   preferences locally on your device.

Q6: Does the app work without internet?
A: You need internet to download episodes and receive updates. However, once
   downloaded, you can listen to episodes offline.

Q7: How often is the app updated?
A: The app receives automatic updates through Firebase. You don't need to
   manually update it.

Q8: Who manages the content?
A: The college administration and designated moderators manage all content to
   ensure accuracy and relevance.

Q9: Can I suggest new features?
A: Absolutely! We welcome suggestions. Contact the admin office or use the
   feedback form on the app.

Q10: What if I forget my access key?
A: Visit the admin office with your ID card and they can generate a new key
    for you.

Q11: Is the app available on Google Play Store?
A: Currently, the app is distributed through direct APK download from the
    college website or admin office. Google Play Store listing is being planned.

Q12: How much storage space does the app need?
A: The app itself is about 33 MB. Downloads depend on how many episodes you
    save.
