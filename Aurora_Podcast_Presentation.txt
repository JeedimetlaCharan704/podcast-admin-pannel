Slide 8: Technical Details
---------------------------
Title: App Specifications

• Platform: Android
• Size: ~33 MB (optimized)
• Internet: Required for live updates
• Offline: Download and listen later
• Updates: Automatic through Firebase

Presenter Notes:
"For those interested in the technical side - the app is built for Android and
is optimized at just 33 MB so it won't take much space on your phone. You need
internet for the latest updates, but once downloaded, episodes work offline too.
The app automatically updates through Firebase cloud service."
