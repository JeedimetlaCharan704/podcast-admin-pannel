SLIDE 2: WHAT IS AURORA PODCAST? (1 minute)
================================================================================

"So, what exactly is Aurora Podcast?

Simply put, it's our college's very own audio communication app. Think of it as
having a personal radio station that broadcasts only college updates, events, and
educational content.

The best part? You can access it right from your Android phone. Whether you're
at home, traveling in the bus, or just taking a walk on campus - you'll never
miss any important update.

This app is completely free for all students and staff of Aurora University."

================================================================================
SLIDE 3: KEY FEATURES (1.5 minutes)
================================================================================

"Let me walk you through the key features.

First, we have INSTANT ANNOUNCEMENTS. Important college notices, exam dates,
holiday announcements - they all reach you immediately.

Then there's the EVENTS section. Never miss a fest, seminar, or any college
activity again. All events are listed with dates, times, and venues.

For our academically inclined students, there's ACADEMIC CONTENT - study
materials, exam updates, and important notices from the examination cell.

But here's the killer feature - OFFLINE LISTENING. You can download any episode
and listen to it later without internet. Perfect for those long bus rides!

The app also sends REAL-TIME NOTIFICATIONS, so you're always in the loop."
